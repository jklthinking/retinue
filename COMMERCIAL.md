仅通过独立接口调用不自动等于衍生作品；具体集成范围应按事实判断。
对未修改的软件提供网络服务，并不单凭托管行为触发第 13 条的修改版义务；
分发软件或客户端时仍须履行适用的 AGPL 条款。

不因核心开源而自动授予访问权。

company, or hosting a service does not by itself require a paid license.

### When to obtain a commercial license

Obtain one before a use for which you cannot or do not wish to meet AGPL's
applicable requirements, such as:

- Distributing a modified version or a derivative product while keeping the
  covered source closed.
- Modifying Retinue and offering it to external users over a network without
  offering those users the Corresponding Source required by AGPL section 13,
  including modifications and necessary build and installation materials.
- Embedding Retinue into a proprietary product that forms a covered combined work.
- Working under a company policy that prohibits AGPL software.

Calling an independent interface does not automatically create a derivative
work; the integration must be assessed on its facts. Hosting an unmodified
copy alone does not trigger section 13's modified-version requirement.
Distribution of software or clients still carries the applicable AGPL duties.

### What the commercial license includes

A signed contract specifies the covered versions, legal entities, term,
deployments and distribution scope. Within that scope, it permits use,
modification, integration or distribution of code that JKL Thinking has the
right to license commercially without that code's AGPL source-disclosure
requirements. Support, SLAs, hosting, enterprise features, upgrades and pricing
are included only when expressly agreed. Hosted offerings and enterprise
features may be supplied from private repositories; the open core grants no
automatic access to them.

Third-party license obligations remain separate. Trademark and logo rights
are not included automatically. This document describes options; it is not an
executed commercial contract. Without such a license, AGPL applies.
